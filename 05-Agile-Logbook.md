##  Overall Reflection on the Agile Logbook
Maintaining this logbook over two weeks was a fantastic learning experience. It made me realize that Agile isn't just about moving cards on a Trello board, it is about daily accountability and open communication. 

During Week 1, my focus was heavily on research and presentation preparation. I learned the importance of observing other teams to improve our own storytelling, and I noted how time management affected our presentation delivery. Moving into Week 2, I took a more active role in setting up our digital tools. Tracking our daily standups forced me to be honest about our team's impediments specifically, that some members were struggling with Trello and that scheduling practice sessions was difficult. My role involved helping the team set up the board and ensuring our daily progress was documented. By writing down these blockers daily, we were able to address them immediately rather than letting them ruin our final presentation. This logbook proved to me that continuous reflection is the key to team success.
